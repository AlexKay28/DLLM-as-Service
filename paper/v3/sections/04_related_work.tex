\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Diffusion LM speedup claims (LLaDA~\cite{llada2025},
DiffusionGemma~\cite{diffusiongemma}) are typically measured against
author-built AR baselines. Constrained decoding
(outlines~\cite{outlines2024}, xgrammar~\cite{xgrammar2024},
llguidance~\cite{llguidance}) is the standard answer to the structured-output
problem and is not measured here; it is a limitation. Speculative decoding
(Medusa~\cite{medusa2024}, EAGLE~\cite{eagle2024}) attacks the same per-token
slope the AR cost law turns on. Harness bias in systems benchmarking is
discussed by~\cite{hoefler2021}.
