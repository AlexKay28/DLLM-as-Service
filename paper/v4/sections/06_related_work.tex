\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Diffusion LM speedup claims (LLaDA~\cite{llada2025},
DiffusionGemma~\cite{diffusiongemma}) are typically measured against
author-built AR baselines. Our contribution is to pin both arms on the
same serving stack and derive cost laws from measurement rather than
from single-point comparisons.

Constrained decoding (outlines~\cite{outlines2024},
xgrammar~\cite{xgrammar2024}, llguidance~\cite{llguidance}) is the
standard answer to the structured-output problem and is not measured
here; it is a stated limitation (\S\ref{sec:threats}). Speculative
decoding (Medusa~\cite{medusa2024}, EAGLE~\cite{eagle2024}) attacks the
same per-token slope the AR cost law turns on; it changes the slope, not
the shape.

\cite{hoefler2021} surveys reproducibility pitfalls in systems
measurement. Our methodological contribution (Appendix~\ref{app:defects})
--- eight priced corrections found by re-reading committed artifacts ---
is a case study in that tradition, not a general method.
