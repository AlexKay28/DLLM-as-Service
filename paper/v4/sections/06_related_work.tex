\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Diffusion LM speedup claims (LLaDA~\cite{llada2025},
DiffusionGemma~\cite{diffusiongemma}) are typically measured against
author-built AR baselines. Constrained decoding
(outlines~\cite{outlines2024}, xgrammar~\cite{xgrammar2024},
llguidance~\cite{llguidance}) is the standard answer to the
structured-output problem and is not measured here; it is a stated
limitation. Speculative decoding (Medusa~\cite{medusa2024},
EAGLE~\cite{eagle2024}) attacks the same per-token slope the AR cost law
turns on.

Harness bias in systems benchmarking has a long history.
\cite{hoefler2021} surveys reproducibility pitfalls in parallel and
distributed systems measurement, including the confounds that arise when
baseline and contender are measured under different stacks. The
asymmetric-failure pattern we report --- where defects cluster on the
arm the experimenter is not rooting for --- is related to confirmation
bias in ML evaluation~\cite{hoefler2021}, but our contribution is
narrower: a checklist that catches the specific failure mode (checkpoint
defaults, parser policy, serving-engine parity, metric semantics,
provenance) in under five minutes per check, using only committed
artifacts. We do not claim generality beyond the structured-output
serving setting we measured.
