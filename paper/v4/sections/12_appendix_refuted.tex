\section{Six refuted hypotheses}
\label{app:refuted} We set out to find regimes where the diffusion model beats the autoregressive
baseline. Six hypotheses were refuted. They are reported at the same length
as the confirmations because the methodological claim --- ``price the baseline
before you compare against it'' --- is spent the moment a negative result
gets softened into a footnote. The distinction from
Appendix~\ref{app:defects}: there the \emph{harness was wrong}; here the
\emph{prediction was wrong}. Table~\ref{tab:refuted} lists all six.
\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Six hypotheses that did not survive measurement
(BGL, $n{=}300$).}
\label{tab:refuted}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{4.6cm}L@{}}
\toprule
\thead{Hypothesis} & \thead{What we measured instead} \\
\midrule
\tabfont Joint denoising resists input perturbation & \tabfont The diffusion model degraded more in \textbf{10 of 10} categories, by up to $8.2\times$ the autoregressive drop. \\
\tabfont Bidirectional conditioning wins at infilling & \tabfont The dense AR-3B scores $0.993$--$0.999$ across all suffix sizes; the diffusion model holds to $0.960$ then falls to $0.753$ at $S{=}8$. \\
\tabfont Diffusion has a refinement mechanism that pays off & \tabfont Selective re-masking gains $+0.023$ F1 over three rounds, but still trails AR after one pass. The mechanism is real; the win is not. \\
\tabfont Diffusion beats AR under bounded deadlines & \tabfont This was defect 4. The AR model does complete within the deadline; the metric had been counting valid falsy values as missing keys. \\
\tabfont Wide-schema cost scales as $O(F^2)$ for diffusion & \tabfont Never measured. The consistency metric is three hardcoded rules over four fields at every schema width. The framing is dropped. \\
\tabfont Quality is invariant to output length & \tabfont Quality rises steeply with $L$ and then plateaus. The apparent invariance came from the contaminated 12-field metric. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Every refuted hypothesis had appeared confirmed at some earlier point, and
each apparent confirmation traced to a harness artifact rather than a
property of either architecture. The measurement discipline is not a
preamble to the results. It is what separates these six from the ones we
kept.
