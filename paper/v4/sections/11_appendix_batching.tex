\section{The batching ceiling}
\label{app:batching} The diffusion batching ceiling ($\leq$\dgBatchVllmHi$\times$) reproduces across
two runtimes and two model families. Both measurements are engine-bounded
(vLLM caps mixed-phase batches at 8; dInfer runs cache-less), so the
true ceiling is unknown but lies at or above \dgBatchVllmHi$\times$.
Disabling prefix caching on the AR arm drops its multiplier from
\cacheOnMult$\times$ to \cacheOffMult$\times$ --- still more than twenty
times the diffusion ceiling. The structural reason is in
Appendix~\ref{app:batching_architectural}.
