\section{Latency distributions}
\label{app:distributions} The per-event latency distributions are asymmetric. Across the 300 individual
events of one anchor run at $L{=}\canvasLength$:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item AR-3B (dense baseline) per-event CV: \arEventCV\%; max/median: \arMaxMed$\times$.
\item DG-26B-A4B per-event CV: \dgEventCV\%; max/median: \dgMaxMed$\times$.
\end{itemize}
The diffusion arm's 3.21$\times$ max/median ratio is driven by
mixture-of-experts routing variability and warm-up on the first events of a
run. Both models are deterministic at temperature 0 --- across five repeats of
300 events, outputs were identical --- so the tail is a property of the
serving path, not of the model's stochasticity. The AR arm's \arEventCV\% CV means
its p50 is a reliable planning number; the DG arm's \dgEventCV\% means a p99 SLA
lands in a very different place than p50 would suggest
(BGL, $n{=}300$).
