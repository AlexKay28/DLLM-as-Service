\section*{Submission disclosures}
\label{app:neurips_checklist}

This version is an article-format manuscript. The venue's current template,
page limits, anonymity policy, and checklist must be applied before
submission. The following disclosures replace the earlier blanket
self-certification; they are not a completed venue-specific checklist.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Claims and limitations.} The main claim concerns measured
serial latency and generation-budget coverage on BGL. Paired scoring shows
a small DG quality deficit against AR-30B. Natural long-output
transfer, optimized constrained/speculative AR, and architectural mechanism
attribution remain unresolved (\S\ref{sec:threats}).
\item \textbf{Theory.} The cost models are empirical approximations.
Their crossover calculation is algebraic and conditional on their fitted
summaries; no architectural lower or upper bound is proved.
\item \textbf{Statistics.} Event-level bootstrap intervals and the
finite-permutation procedure are reported for the primary medians. A new
post-hoc paired bootstrap reanalyzes 300 saved outputs without new inference.
Only AR has three timing seeds; event dependence and run effects are not
fully controlled. Extreme-tail statistics are exploratory.
\item \textbf{Code and data.} Experiment code, result files, and
provenance are in the repository. The v9 audits cover named claims;
passing it does not validate all experimental protocols. Upstream models
and datasets are cited, and their revision-specific licenses should be
recorded with the released environment.
\item \textbf{Resources.} The principal experiments use one A100 80GB;
the present reanalysis uses saved outputs and no new GPU inference.
\item \textbf{Assets and human subjects.} The study releases experiment
code and result artifacts rather than a new trained model. It uses public
data and involves no human-subjects research.
\item \textbf{Broader impact.} Lower serving latency may reduce resource
requirements, but does not establish correctness or suitability for
high-stakes deployment.
\item \textbf{LLM assistance.} An LLM-based assistant helped review and
edit the manuscript, check claims against sources, and prepare v8 and v9. The
previous statement that no LLM was used for writing or editing is not
applicable to this version. Authors should verify the final submission.
\end{itemize}
