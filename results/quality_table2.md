## Caveats

1. **Model substitution**: we use open Qwen2.5 models as AR substitutes, not the paper's exact AR-1.5B/AR-7B. F1 differences reflect model + prompt differences, not just the diffusion-vs-AR question.
2. **Gold labels**: semantic fields (event_type, affected_entity, remediation_hint) are self-generated placeholders. AR models that produce real semantic labels will be scored against weak gold — their semantic F1 may appear artificially low.
3. **FlowFill column**: blocked on weights. Paper values shown for reference.
4. **Single corpus**: only BGL. Paper averages 4 corpora.
