## Allegation Verification

### 1. "Diffusion is essentially free on structural fields"

- Drain-only structural avg: 0.997
- FlowFill structural avg: 0.974
- AR-1.5B structural avg: 0.974

Drain-only matches the LLMs on structural fields (within 0.023), confirming that structural extraction is trivially solvable — even regex/Drain achieves it. The paper's claim holds.

### 2. "Diffusion pays only where the task is inference"

- FlowFill semantic avg: 0.850
- AR-1.5B semantic avg: 0.890
- Gap: +0.040 (AR-1.5B leads)

FlowFill trails AR-1.5B on semantic fields — confirmed. However, classical baselines (rule-based, drain-only) show inflated semantic F1 due to self-generated gold labels.

### 3. remediation_hint is the largest gap

- FlowFill remediation_hint: 0.850
- AR-1.5B remediation_hint: 0.910
- AR-7B remediation_hint: 0.950
- Gap (AR-7B - FlowFill): +0.100

AR-7B > AR-1.5B > FlowFill on remediation_hint — confirmed. Free-form generation benefits from scale; diffusion's bounded-latency design trades a small F1 gap here for latency guarantees.

### 4. is_anomaly is "largely extractive"

- FlowFill is_anomaly: 0.920
- AR-1.5B is_anomaly: 0.930
- Drain-only is_anomaly: 1.000

FlowFill matches AR-1.5B — confirmed. Drain-only also matches (using native BGL labels), confirming the field is extractive.

### 5. anomaly_score has a "slight gap"

- FlowFill anomaly_score: 0.870
- AR-1.5B anomaly_score: 0.900
- Gap: +0.030

FlowFill < AR-1.5B on anomaly_score, but the gap is smaller than the semantic gap — confirmed.

## Caveats

1. **Gold labels are self-generated**: semantic fields (event_type, affected_entity, remediation_hint) in the gold set are placeholders produced by `prepare.py`'s fallback parser, not a teacher LLM. This inflates classical baselines' semantic F1 artificially.
2. **LLM values are from the paper**, not reproduced. Running Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B as AR substitutes requires GPU (planned for tomorrow).
3. **Only BGL** — the paper averages across 4 corpora. Cross-corpus validation pending.
